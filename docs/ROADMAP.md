# DreamCart — Master Roadmap (current state → public MVP)

**Status:** Phase 0 deliverable (added in the self-review). Supersedes the PRD roadmap table and Blueprint §8 numbering. Sizes are relative (S/M/L), not calendar estimates. Claude works on **one phase at a time**; every phase ends in a pull request that only the owner merges. Owner-side tracks (RT-*) and owner reviews overlap with the next phase.

## How to read this

- **Tracks:** *Research* (RT) = product research and data validation, human-led. Phases are tagged *Spec* (architecture and specifications), *Foundation* (engineering foundation), *Discovery* (product discovery and catalog), *UI/UX*, *Engine* (virtual shopping engine), *QA/Sec* (testing and security), *Launch* (deployment and launch).
- **Shared Definition of Done (every phase):** acceptance criteria met; tests pass locally and in CI; build passes; no critical or high defect open; no secrets and no paid service; docs and ADRs updated for any deviation; pull request lists changed files and validation evidence and is approved by the owner. Phase-specific additions appear under "DoD+".
- **Scope control:** anything outside `docs/PRD.md` §4 needs owner approval before it enters a phase. No phase may add ratings, discounts, popularity signals, or engagement mechanics.

## Order and critical path

Execution order: **P0 → P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 → P5 → P6 → P7 → P8 → P9 → P10 → P11 → P12.**

```
P0 approval ─► P1 ─► P2 design ──► P3 domain ─► P4 persistence ─┐
   │                (owner reviews P2 while Claude builds P3/P4)  ├─► P6 ─► P7 ─► P8 ─► P9 ─► P10 ─► P11 ─► P12
   └─► RT-2 ─► RT-3 batch 1 ─► P5 catalog pipeline ───────────────┘
         (RT-1, RT-4 alongside; RT-3 batches 2–3 and RT-5 feed P6, P11, P12)
```

The longest and least certain items: **RT-2/RT-3** (image rights and manual curation: human, external, unpredictable), then **P2 sign-off** (owner time), then **P6** (largest build). Engineering P1, P3, P4 is predictable and never needs to wait on data. If RT-2/RT-3 slip, P1–P4 still proceed on synthetic fixtures; P5 and P6 wait.

## Research and data validation track (human-led, alongside phases)

### RT-1 Provider terms reading
1. **Objective:** replace UNKNOWN entries in `PRODUCT_SOURCE_MATRIX.md` with primary-source evidence. 2. **Scope:** Shopify Global Catalog, Open Icecat, Commons, manufacturer terms; eBay/Amazon/Lazada only if a future phase needs them. 3. **Tasks:** allow the blocked hosts or read manually; record clause, URL, date; decide PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN per dimension. 4. **Deliverables:** updated matrix. 5. **Depends:** network allowlist or owner reading. 6. **Acceptance:** every PASS/FAIL cites a clause. 7. **Tests:** reviewer spot-checks 3 citations. 8. **DoD+:** none. 9. **Risks:** terms change; ambiguity needs legal advice. 10. **Approval:** Yes. Not required for the curated MVP.

### RT-2 Image rights and acquisition plan (critical)
1. **Objective:** a lawful image source for every shipped product, or an explicit placeholder decision. 2. **Scope:** chosen catalog brands; Commons, Open Icecat, manufacturer permission, owner photography; `brand_art`. 3. **Tasks:** per brand find published press/image terms; request written permission where unclear; record `MediaAsset.rights` with evidence; apply the P-4 strategy (placeholders where nothing is usable). 4. **Deliverables:** private evidence archive and rights records. 5. **Depends:** product shortlist (RT-3). 6. **Acceptance:** each curated product is `CLEARED` with evidence or has a recorded placeholder decision. 7. **Tests:** schema validation (P5). 8. **DoD+:** owner signs the rights records. 9. **Risks:** premium brands refuse or ignore; replies slow; may force a different product mix. 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### RT-3 Catalog curation
1. **Objective:** 20–30 verified premium products. 2. **Scope:** laptops/computers, smartphones, headphones/audio. 3. **Tasks:** follow `CURATION_PROCESS.md`; batch 1 (≥10, all categories) before P5; batch 2 (20) before P6 polish; batch 3 (up to 30) before the pilot. 4. **Deliverables:** completed CSV rows, private evidence. 5. **Depends:** RT-2. 6. **Acceptance:** each entry meets "verified entry". 7. **Tests:** the P5 validator. 8. **DoD+:** second check done. 9. **Risks:** B1; data going stale before launch. 10. **Approval:** Yes (the owner curates or signs off).

### RT-4 Editorial content and brand art
1. **Objective:** copy and visuals for the editorial home. 2. **Scope:** Vietnamese copy deck, hero stories, abstract `brand_art`. 3. **Tasks:** author in own words from catalog facts; produce or commission brand art. 4. **Deliverables:** content files. 5. **Depends:** P2 direction. 6. **Acceptance:** no unsourced claim; assets are `own_work`. 7. **Tests:** content schema validation. 8. **DoD+:** owner approves copy. 9. **Risks:** time; tone drift. 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### RT-5 Legal and terms check before public launch
1. **Objective:** owner-level confidence for public use. 2. **Scope:** price-snapshot basis (P-3), image terms, privacy notice, hosting terms (noncommercial status). 3. **Tasks:** owner or counsel review; finalize notices. 4. **Deliverables:** sign-off note. 5. **Depends:** RT-1/2/3. 6. **Acceptance:** written owner decision. 7. **Tests:** n/a. 8. **DoD+:** notices match the data inventory. 9. **Risks:** a finding that forces catalog changes. 10. **Approval:** Yes (blocks P12).

## Phases

### P0 Discovery and specification — *Spec* — DONE (merge authorized by the owner)
1. **Objective:** decide feasibility and freeze requirements. 2. **Scope:** documents, fixtures, verification tool. 3. **Tasks:** PRD, architecture, schema, matrix, curation process, fixtures, ADRs, entry criteria; self-review and remediation. 4. **Deliverables:** PR #1. 5. **Depends:** none. 6. **Acceptance:** owner reviews; no unknown right shown as permitted; verifier passes. 7. **Tests:** `node tools/verify-fixtures.mjs`, `node tools/check-docs.mjs`. 8. **DoD+:** decisions P-1…P-10 recorded in `DECISIONS.md`. 9. **Risks:** B1–B3, B5. 10. **Approval:** Yes (merging is the owner's).

### P1 Engineering Foundation — *Foundation* — M — IMPLEMENTED (pull request open for owner review)
1. **Objective:** a guarded, empty, runnable project. 2. **Scope:** tooling, CI, guardrails; no product features. 3. **Tasks:** scaffold Next.js (App Router, TypeScript strict) with Tailwind and a shadcn/ui foundation on Node 24 LTS and npm (lockfile committed); ESLint boundaries; Prettier; Vitest; npm scripts that also run the Phase 0 fixture and docs checks; GitHub Actions (`npm ci`, lint, typecheck, test, build, fixtures, docs); `platform/i18n/vi.ts` and `t()`; semantic design tokens; minimal shell and route skeletons; module skeleton with a domain-purity test; security headers and CSP evaluation against the pinned Next.js; README run instructions. 4. **Deliverables:** app shell with simulation banner, CI green, ADR-011 updated with the spike result. 5. **Depends:** P0 merged. 6. **Acceptance:** lint, typecheck, test, build, fixture, and docs checks pass locally and in CI; adding a React import to `domain` fails the test; security headers present and the CSP decision documented; basic routes render; no third-party runtime requests. Playwright is introduced once real journeys exist. 7. **Tests:** unit tests (boundary, dictionary, URL safety, headers, navigation config) and route render checks. 8. **DoD+:** versions recorded. 9. **Risks:** version drift (TypeScript is held on 6.0 for `typescript-eslint`); CSP tradeoffs. 10. **Approval:** Yes (CSP choice).

### P2 UI/UX Design System — *UI/UX* — M
1. **Objective:** owner-approved visual and interaction design before the storefront build. 2. **Scope:** design, tokens, a component gallery; no product screens. 3. **Tasks:** evaluate and integrate `nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill` (P-7: inspect its actual files and license, verify Claude Code Cloud compatibility, prefer a version-controlled project-level integration, pin a reviewed commit, and treat its recommendations as subordinate to DreamCart requirements; **not installed before Phase 2**); visual direction (Apple-inspired, DreamCart identity, U1 guardrails); open-license typography and license check; color, spacing, motion; wordmark and `brand_art` direction; designs with all states for editorial home, search and discovery, product detail with the Hybrid Continuous Configurator (P-5: all groups on one page, collapsible sections, revisitable selections, quick navigation, sticky summary, reset flow, disabled-option reasons), cart drawer, checkout, confirmation, tracking, collection, settings and export; simulation-label pattern; Vietnamese copy deck; Tailwind tokens; gallery route. 4. **Deliverables:** `docs/UX_SPEC.md`, design assets in the repository, tokens, gallery. 5. **Depends:** P1 (tokens, gallery); RT-2 shapes the imagery. 6. **Acceptance:** owner signs off in writing; every screen has empty/loading/error states; token contrast meets AA. 7. **Tests:** automated contrast check; axe on the gallery. 8. **DoD+:** skill evaluation recorded. 9. **Risks:** drift toward imitation; font licensing; scope creep; skill supply chain. 10. **Approval:** Yes. Sign-off is a hard gate for P6; the owner reviews while Claude builds P3/P4.

### P3 Domain & Simulation Core — *Engine* — L
1. **Objective:** every business rule as pure, tested code that passes the fixtures. 2. **Scope:** domain and application logic; no UI, no persistence. 3. **Tasks:** Zod schemas; money and currency exponents; price state and eligibility (with the P-1 configuration); search parse/match/classify; configurator derivation; cart; snapshot builder; simulation function and scenarios; URL validator and `SafeUrl`; idempotency-key logic; port interfaces; convert `tools/verify-fixtures.mjs` into Vitest suites and delete the tool; add property tests (random clock sequences never regress state). 4. **Deliverables:** modules, suites, coverage report. 5. **Depends:** P1; P-1 and P-2 (decided). 6. **Acceptance:** G2, G3, G4, G11 (validator); simulation boundary and cancel-race tests pass; `domain` imports nothing forbidden. 7. **Tests:** unit, property, and the fixtures in `fixtures/`. 8. **DoD+:** the old verification tool is removed. 9. **Risks:** rules too rigid for real vocabularies (mitigated by real-catalog fixtures in P5). 10. **Approval:** Yes; any rule change goes through the owner.

### P4 Persistence & Import Foundation — *Engine* — M
1. **Objective:** durable, atomic, exportable storage. 2. **Scope:** IndexedDB adapters and data-control features. 3. **Tasks:** schema v1 and migrations; adapters for the three ports; transactional `placeOrder`/`cancel`/`advance`; cart `revision` and `checkoutKey`; quarantine; hardened export/import; persistent-storage request and status; memory fallback and banner; "had data, now empty" marker; delete order and reset all. 4. **Deliverables:** adapters and integration tests. 5. **Depends:** P3. 6. **Acceptance:** G7, G8 (storage), G9; N parallel `placeOrder` calls yield one order; hostile import files rejected; reset removes everything. 7. **Tests:** Vitest with fake IndexedDB; **real-browser** two-tab race on Chromium, Firefox, WebKit; migration test. 8. **DoD+:** the data inventory matches `ARCHITECTURE.md` §9. 9. **Risks:** fake IndexedDB differs from real browsers; Safari quirks. 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### P5 Real Product Catalog & Search — *Discovery* — M
1. **Objective:** a real, validated catalog feeding working search. 2. **Scope:** data pipeline and search over real data; no UI. 3. **Tasks:** data layout; CSV→JSON converter and Zod validator (rights, required attributes, `fx-` guard, advisory staleness report); image pipeline with size budgets and attribution extraction; build-time loader with `catalogVersion` hash; per-category vocabularies from real data; facet counts; real-catalog fixtures approved by the owner; first ≥10 verified entries (RT-3 batch 1); dev-only toggle for the fixture catalog. 4. **Deliverables:** `data/catalog/`, scripts, loader, credits data. 5. **Depends:** P1, P3; RT-2 and RT-3 batch 1. 6. **Acceptance:** G5 (data), G6; the build fails for each class of invalid record; real-catalog search fixtures pass; no `fx-` in a production bundle. 7. **Tests:** validator tests with invalid-record fixtures; build-output guard; facet tests. 8. **DoD+:** rights records signed by the owner. 9. **Risks:** B1, B2; vocabulary mismatch; repository size. 10. **Approval:** Yes (data PR).

### P6 Storefront Implementation — *UI/UX* — L (two pull requests: 6a shell, home, search; 6b product page and configurator)
1. **Objective:** polished discovery and product pages on real data. 2. **Scope:** `/`, `/search`, `/product/[id]`, `/credits`, `/about-simulation`. 3. **Tasks:** header with persistent search and suggestions; simulation label; editorial home from content files; results grid, filters with counts, sort, chips, empty states, match/near/ambiguous presentation; gallery, specs, provenance and offers, outbound notice; hybrid configurator per spec; image components with placeholder; skeletons; reduced motion; responsive; Vietnamese strings; credits page. 4. **Deliverables:** the routes above. 5. **Depends:** P2 approved; P3; P5. 6. **Acceptance:** G1 (these routes), G2/G4 in the UI, G5, G6, G12 (automated); visual QA against the spec at desktop and mobile. 7. **Tests:** component tests; Playwright flow search → filter → product → configure; axe; **banned-pattern test** (no ratings, sales, discount, or urgency elements); optional screenshots. 8. **DoD+:** owner visual review. 9. **Risks:** largest build; imagery rights; image performance. 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### P7 Cart & Manual Import — *Engine* — M
1. **Objective:** items reach a durable cart. 2. **Scope:** import page, cart, header count. 3. **Tasks:** URL validate/clean/confirm and manual form; `userProducts` storage with unverified labels; cart drawer and page; add acknowledgement with live region; eligibility reasons; subtotals grouped by source; quantity controls; corrupted-data and storage notices. 4. **Deliverables:** `/import`, `/cart`. 5. **Depends:** P4, P6. 6. **Acceptance:** J3, J4, G3 (UI), G11, G9 (import path). 7. **Tests:** Playwright for add/merge/limit, refresh, two tabs, malicious URLs, hostile import file through the UI. 8. **DoD+:** none. 9. **Risks:** import UX tedium. 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### P8 Virtual Checkout & Orders — *Engine* — M
1. **Objective:** one transparent, idempotent virtual order. 2. **Scope:** checkout, confirmation, orders, data-control UI. 3. **Tasks:** checkout page (summary, scenario, destination, payment label, acknowledgement); idempotent confirm; confirmation with watermark and `DC-V-` code; orders list and detail from snapshots with image re-check; cancel by state; delete order, reset all, export/import UI. 4. **Deliverables:** `/checkout/simulated`, `/orders`, `/orders/[id]`, `/settings`. 5. **Depends:** P4, P7. 6. **Acceptance:** J5, J8, G1 (all surfaces), G7, G10 up to confirmation. 7. **Tests:** Playwright journeys on all projects; zero cross-origin requests; per-route label test; export then import in a clean context. 8. **DoD+:** copy reviewed so nothing can pass as a receipt. 9. **Risks:** receipt-like appearance; snapshot gaps. 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### P9 Virtual Tracking & My Collection — *Engine* — M
1. **Objective:** the simulated journey and My Collection. 2. **Scope:** timeline, Fast control, delivered state, collection. 3. **Tasks:** timeline UI per scenario; Fast "next step"; display timer for time-based updates; high-water writes on view; delivered screen; collection gallery rendered from snapshots; in-app notice on open; offline behavior. 4. **Deliverables:** `/orders/[id]` timeline, `/collection`. 5. **Depends:** P8. 6. **Acceptance:** J6, J7, G8. 7. **Tests:** Playwright with `page.clock`: Quick and Relaxed progress, reload, clock moved back, cancel at each state; each delivered item once. 8. **DoD+:** none. 9. **Risks:** timer drift in background tabs (display only). 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### P10 Comprehensive QA, Security and Performance — *QA/Sec* — M/L
1. **Objective:** make it safe, accessible, fast, and robust, including the one comprehensive system-wide audit (earlier phases get only a concise self-review). 2. **Scope:** no new features. 3. **Tasks:** verify CSP and headers on the real build; manual accessibility pass (keyboard and a screen reader); performance work to G13; real-device tests (iOS Safari, Android Chrome) including storage eviction checks; full browser-matrix run; security review of import, URL, and XSS surfaces; dependency and license audit; final privacy notice; error boundaries; failure injection (IndexedDB disabled, quota, corruption); runbook and backup guide. 4. **Deliverables:** reports, fixes, runbook. 5. **Depends:** P9. 6. **Acceptance:** G10–G13; no critical or high security finding; eviction findings recorded with a decision on pulling sync forward. 7. **Tests:** full e2e matrix, axe, performance lab runs, security tests. 8. **DoD+:** residual risks listed and accepted by the owner. 9. **Risks:** late discovery of platform storage limits. 10. **Approval:** Yes (risk acceptance).

### P11 Pilot Testing and UX Polish — *QA/Sec* — M, human-heavy
1. **Objective:** validate usability and comprehension. 2. **Scope:** 5–10 volunteers; fixes for validated friction and bugs only. 3. **Tasks:** recruit, consent, no personal data; task script; comprehension question; triage; fix; regression run. 4. **Deliverables:** pilot report, changelog. 5. **Depends:** P10; RT-3 batch 3. 6. **Acceptance:** G14; all severity-1 and 2 findings fixed. 7. **Tests:** regression suite. 8. **DoD+:** owner go/no-go. 9. **Risks:** recruitment; scope creep from feedback. 10. **Approval:** Yes.

### P12 Optional Public MVP Launch — *Launch* — S/M
1. **Objective:** a public, free-tier, clearly simulated release. 2. **Scope:** deploy and document. 3. **Tasks:** confirm current hosting terms; owner connects the hosting account on a free plan; preview-deployment checks (headers, smoke); optional domain; final credits, privacy, and about pages; launch checklist; verified rollback plan; bugfix-only cadence afterward. 4. **Deliverables:** live URL, launch checklist, changelog. 5. **Depends:** P11; RT-5; RT-2 complete. 6. **Acceptance:** complete journey works on target browsers on the deployed URL; simulation notice on every route; no paid resource; rollback verified. 7. **Tests:** smoke suite against the deployment. 8. **DoD+:** owner signs the launch checklist. 9. **Risks:** hosting-terms mismatch; unread terms (B3); image complaints (keep a takedown path). 10. **Approval:** Yes, explicit launch authorization; deployment is never pre-authorized.
