# ADR-008: Psychological benefits are hypotheses; research is opt-in only

**Status:** Accepted (carried from Blueprint v1.0)
**Date:** 2026-10-29

## Context
The Blueprint's evidence ledger describes studies that do not validate DreamCart. Citations were not independently re-verified in Phase 0.

## Decision
Product copy makes no therapeutic, clinical, or compulsive-shopping claims. Any future research module is consent-based, pre-registered, and needs its own ethics and privacy plan and ADR. No behavioral analytics by default. The tagline "Shop the feeling. Not the spending." is a positioning line, not a health claim.

## Consequences
Marketing and onboarding copy is reviewed against this rule. Engagement time is not a success metric.
